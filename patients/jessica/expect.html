When to call us

Anything that worries you is worth a message — you will never be a bother. Reach out the same day for:
  • Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or trouble breathing
  • Severe or persistent abdominal pain
  • Vomiting that stops you keeping fluids down
  • A reaction at the injection site that spreads, or is hot and painful
  • Losing more than 3–4 lb in a week

If you cannot breathe, your throat is closing, or you feel faint — call 911 first. Tell us afterwards.
