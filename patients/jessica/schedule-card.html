Changing your schedule

If your start day moves, or you would rather inject at a different hour.

On your card

  1. 1 Tap your Atelier card and open Today’s Ritual.
  2. 2 Tap Started… — change near the top.
  3. 3 Set the day you started and the hour for each pen, then tap Set my schedule. Your app reminders follow straight away. Your calendar does not — so do the two steps below.

In your calendar

  1. 4 Tap Remove from calendar, then open the file your phone downloads. This clears the doses from the old schedule.
  2. 5 Tap Add to calendar and open that file. When your phone asks where to put them, choose a calendar of their own named Atelier Doses.

To clear every dose at once — in your Calendar app, delete the calendar named Atelier Doses. Nothing else of yours is in it.
