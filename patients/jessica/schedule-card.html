Your dosing calendar

Subscribe once, and it keeps itself right.

Setting it up — once

  1. 1 Tap your Atelier card, open Today’s Ritual, and tap Subscribe in Calendar. Say yes. Keep alerts on if you would like your calendar to remind you too.

Changing your schedule

  1. 2 On Today’s Ritual, tap Started… — change.
  2. 3 Set the day you started and the hour for each pen, then tap Set my schedule. That is all. Your reminders and your calendar both follow — there is nothing to re-add and nothing left over from the old plan.

To remove every dose — open Calendar, tap Calendars, tap the i beside Atelier Doses, then Delete Calendar. Nothing else of yours is in it.
