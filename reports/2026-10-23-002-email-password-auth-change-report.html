What was built

The first-tap card gate now offers email + password alongside Google. A new patient creates an account right on the card (name, email, password), the card links to that account exactly once, and a forgotten password is recovered through Firebase's reset email, which returns the patient to the card. Everything about SPEC-001's link-once model stays: later taps verify only, a different account is blocked with a quiet stuck screen, and the atelier still sees who activated each card.

Behavior changes

  • The welcome gate's options — before: One button: Sign in with Google · after: Google button plus a 'Use email instead' path
  • Sign-in failure message — before: n/a (no email sign-in) · after: one uniform message — never reveals whether the email or the password was wrong
  • Forgotten password — before: contact the atelier · after: reset email with a link that returns the patient to the card
  • Stuck screen copy — before: 'It belongs to a different account. Message us.' · after: provider-agnostic — points the patient at whichever method they used first
