Vial stickers

One address per peptide, not per patient — write a roll of each and any sticker works for whoever is holding the pen. Tapping one asks “log today’s dose?” and marks the day on that patient’s calendar. Add a lot number if you want it recorded with every dose from that box.

Naming a patient puts her card in the sticker, so tapping it works on any phone with nothing remembered — no “open your card first”. Use it for a vial dispensed to one person. Leave it blank for a roll that goes on whatever comes out of the fridge.
